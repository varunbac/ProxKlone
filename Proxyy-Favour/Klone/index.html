HOW TO PLAY

OBJECTIVE

Reach the glowing exit portal in every level. Clones copy your moves according to their own rule. Solve the puzzle in as few moves and as fast as possible.

1. Exit Portal

Step on it to finish the level. Clones cannot finish for you.

2. Turn-Based

Every step = 1 move. Clones react right after you move.

3. Star Par

Meet par moves and par time to earn up to 3 stars.

THE 5 CLONE MECHANICS

1. Swap Clone (L1)

Mirrors moves. Press Q to swap. Press F on a plate to freeze.

2. Freeze Clone (L2)

Moves ONLY while holding Shift. Press F on a plate to freeze.

3. Shy Clone (L3)

Steps 1 tile away when close. Press F on a plate to freeze.

4. Magnet Clone (L4)

Mirrors moves and drags crates. Press F on a plate to freeze.

5. Sacrifice Clone (L5)

Mirrors moves. Press X next to it to destroy, or F on a plate to freeze.

PRESSURE PLATES & BOARD MECHANICS

Pressure Plates

Count matches clone count. Active while held down or when a clone is locked on it using F key.

Crates

Push 1 tile into empty space to hold plates permanently.

Doors & Lasers

Stay open/disabled while linked plates are pressed.

Exit Portal

Unlocks when all linked door requirements are fulfilled.

PRO TIPS & KLONE AI

1Press F when a clone reaches a pressure plate to freeze it down securely.
2Check scrambled wall layouts and ensure all matching plates are held down.
